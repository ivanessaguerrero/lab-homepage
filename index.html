Isabel's Tattoos & Their Meanings

Since turning 18, I have spent 1,000 dollars on tattoos! Now, to justify that kind of spending, I made sure that each piece is representative of an important part of my life. For example, the first tattoo I ever got was a cursive G. It stands for Guerrero, which is my last name, but more importantly, it reminds me of the relationship I had with my grandfather. Now, let's delve into the other 4 meanings behind the ink.

  • Above and Beyond (inner wrist) - a sentinment my dad had led me with, especially during my academic and professional endeavors.
  • Cross (inner forearm) - represents my faith and the strength my mother has taught me through our religious beliefs.
  • 1996 (inner bicep) - the year my parents met and began their lives together.
  • "In God's Hands" (inner forearm) - a quote in my mother's handwriting that reminds of me the difficult times she has guided me through.
