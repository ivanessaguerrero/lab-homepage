Featured Tattoo: "G"

I got my G tattoo on my 18th Birthday! I had always wanted one that was simple, yet powerful. However, when I woke up freshly 18, I did not anticipate walking into a tattoo shop that day.

Every year, my parents take me to a restraunt in Monterey, CA for my birthday. This year wasn't any different. We drove the Monterey, reserved my favorite booth, and ate lunch as we overlooked the water. All of a sudden I kept nameing off all the things I could now do as a legal adult. One of them being "get a tattoo!" As those words left me mouth, I remembered seeing a tattoo shop on the way over. It was almost like my parents could see my thoughts formulating. They looked at each other then asked me "what would you get" I said, "a G for grandpa Manuel." After we finished up our lunch, we headed over to the shop, and walked out with my first tattoo.

Now, every time I see it, I'm reminded of the life my grandpa wanted from me and I can't help but think of how proud he is of what I have achieved at Claremont McKenna and beyond.

This tattoo took under 15 minutes to complete, start to finish.
